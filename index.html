      Community preview · No live members
      Try the experience with fictional details. Stories below are illustrative examples. Your posts, replies and actions stay in this page’s memory and disappear on reload. No one else sees them. No account or moderator is connected.
      Our Circle agreements
      • Listen with kindness. No judgement, harassment, hate or body shaming.
      • Share experiences rather than diagnoses, medication instructions or promises of a cure.
      • Keep names, addresses, contact details and identifying information out of stories, including other people’s information.
      • No pressure to share, no explicit images and no promotional or money requests.
      • In a live community, anonymous display would hide your profile from members, not necessarily from authorised moderators. That system is not built here.

      Live posting needs accounts, access controls, a privacy policy, trained moderation and working report/block services. This preview does not provide them.

      Care & Safety update · V6.1

      Choose the person’s current location, not their nationality. We do not detect or save it.

      If there is immediate danger

      Contact the emergency service where the person needing help is located. Do not wait for a reply from this app or a community post.

      What kind of support?

      Choose a starting point. This does not assess symptoms or decide how urgent something is.

      Privacy, sources & coverage

      This is a small independent directory for five countries, not worldwide coverage. Urgent clinical advice, listening support, official information and ongoing-treatment resources are labelled separately. Source pages were read on 15 November 2026. Phone lines were not test-called. Direct overseas crisis-line contacts are not yet included; follow official support pages for current local options. A source check does not guarantee a service is open, available or suitable now.

      Calls and external websites can leave records on your phone or browser. Use a safer device if needed. Quick exit opens a blank page; it does not clear history, stored app data, downloads or call records.

      No account or AI chat is needed for Care. AdwoaVelle cannot contact services for you, monitor your safety or supply medical care. Its chat and community remain demos.

      Current directory entries include English-language information. Provider translation, accessibility, charges and eligibility should be checked on the service website.
